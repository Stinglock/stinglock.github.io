Highsec missions — 2 to 4

The one that already works with zero coordination. Anyone pairs with anyone — Stinglock's Hurricane/Maelstrom, Miko's Raven/Drake/Caracal, or whatever Chezz and Cody bring. Fleet up so bounties share, no shared hulls or skill overlap required.

Cost to enter: free, but L3+ agents need standing each pilot builds individually — nobody can piggyback on someone else's rep. Time: 15–30 minutes per mission depending on level. ISK/hr: roughly ~15-30M/hr at L1–L2 per pilot, climbing to ~60-110M/hr at L4 in a proper battleship. Risk: very low — CONCORD-protected, the only real threat is a highsec ganker targeting an expensive-looking fit sitting still too long.

Faction Warfare — 1 to 4

Plexing for LP and territory in the Minmatar/Amarr (or relevant) warzone. The Jaguar is already owned and built for exactly this, but it's sat idle all session — nobody's actually run FW content yet.

Cost to enter: free, just militia enlistment and a modest standing requirement. Time: a plex typically runs 15–20 minutes uncontested, longer if the opposing militia shows up and it turns into a fight. ISK/hr: LP-driven rather than bounty-driven, roughly ~20-50M/hr equivalent once LP is turned into ISK, but swings hard with system tier and market LP-store prices. Risk: high — FW space is lowsec by design, so it's real PvP against the opposing militia plus neutral gate campers, not a safe grind.

Incursions — 1 or more, public fleet

Sansha incursion fleets are community-run, not something the four of us need to organise ourselves — anyone can join independently in a properly fit battleship. The Maelstrom and Typhoon are already the right hull class.

Cost to enter: free beyond owning a suitable battleship and being in the fleet's comms/channel. Time: individual sites clear in 10–20 minutes once a fleet's established and moving. ISK/hr: ~80-150M/hr per pilot, among the best steady highsec/lowsec ISK/hr available. Risk: very low — large organised fleets with logistics support, the safest high-payout content on this page.

Nullsec relic via filament — 2 to 4

Full run-through on Exploration. One Noise-5 filament (~65-70k ISK) carries the whole group in on one activation.

Cost to enter: ~65-70k ISK total for the group, one filament. Time: 2.5–4 hours per session, most of it scanning. ISK/hr: ~100-250M/hr per pilot before travel/scanning overhead eats into it. Risk: moderate — no local-chat-equivalent warning, unknown system on arrival.

Gas harvesting — 1+, rides along with the explorers

The actual fit for a mining-focused pilot. Gas sites turn up in the same C1–C3 holes the group is already scanning into for relic sites — a Venture (or Prospect once trained) harvests them independently while the explorers hack cans on the same visible, no combat skills or Minmatar hulls needed.

Cost to enter: free — a Venture is cheap and disposable, and one's already owned in the fleet. Time: a gas site depletes in roughly 20–40 minutes depending on how many are harvesting it. ISK/hr: ~30-80M/hr solo, swings with which gas type and current market price. Risk: moderate — same wormhole hazards as everything else in this space (no local, unknown residents), but the Venture itself is cheap to lose and carries a built-in warp core strength bonus.

Cody's actual role

This is the one item on the whole page that doesn't need a single Minmatar skill or a shared hull — just a Venture and a willingness to sit in the same hole the explorers are working. Good starting slot once he's got an export on file.

C1–C3 Sleeper sites — 2 to 3

Loki shoots (Stinglock), Noctis salvages (Stinglock), a cloaky frigate watches local/d-scan. With only one pilot holding Minmatar ship skills, this currently means one person rotating between roles across trips rather than three people running it live — or a second/third person tagging along in an unrelated ship just to watch, which still adds real value per the safety notes on Exploration.

Cost to enter: free — the Loki, Cyclone and Noctis are already owned and paid for. Time: a full session (scan, clear, salvage, scan a chain home) runs 2–3 hours. ISK/hr: ~100-150M/hr net per pilot once travel and hole control are deducted from the raw per-site payout. Risk: moderate–high — no local chat, and the Loki's slow align time means a mistake is expensive if a hunter lands on grid.

C4+ Sleeper sites — 3 to 4

Aspirational, not current

From C4 up there are no unguarded faction sites left — everything is Sleeper-guarded and harder, which is genuinely group content. It needs at least two real combat ships plus salvage and watch, and right now the crew fields exactly one combat-capable Minmatar pilot. Revisit once a second person has a hull and the skills to fight alongside the Loki.

Cost to enter: free, same hulls as C1–C3, but the sites want more dps/tank to clear reliably. Time: longer sessions, 3–5 hours, deeper wormhole chains to reach and leave. ISK/hr: ~200-400M/hr per pilot — blue loot value climbs sharply past C3. Risk: high — harder Sleeper spawns, deeper in hostile space, no unguarded fallback content if a site goes badly.

Abyssal Deadspace — 1, solo mechanic

Entry is a solo constraint, not a group one — only a single T1/T2/navy/pirate cruiser (T3 barred, so no Loki) or a matched pair of destroyers or trio of frigates can go in on one filament. As a group, this means taking turns rather than running it together: whoever's turn it is gets the run, the others watch or do something else.

Cost to enter: ~10k ISK per filament, trivial. Time: hard 20-minute cap per run, no exceptions — overstay and the ship and pod are both destroyed by the environment itself. Risk: high — death is total (ship + pod), not a "warp out and try again" situation like everything else on this page. Ready now: the Rupture or Stabber both qualify today, corrected from an earlier version of the Content page that wrongly said this needed a Triglavian hull.

Small-gang PvP roams — 2 to 4

Genuinely available — Hurricane and Loki are both real PvP-capable hulls — but nobody's built a PvP fit or practiced this yet. Different skillset from PvE: tackle, target calling, disengage discipline.

Cost to enter: free beyond the ships themselves. Time: a roam runs however long the group wants, 1–3 hours typical. Payout: killmails and occasional loot, not steady ISK/hr — this is content for its own sake, not income. Risk: high by design — that's the point of PvP.

Nullsec sov warfare — needs a coalition

Not on this crew's scale

Sovereignty warfare is fought by alliances holding or contesting territory, with the infrastructure (citadels, upgrades, standing fleets) that only a much larger organisation maintains. A 5-person corp participates in this by joining an existing alliance's structure, not by running it independently. Not something to plan around unless the corp's whole direction changes.

Capitals & structures — needs a bigger corp

Alliance-scale, genuinely far off

Capital ships and citadel ownership both need resources and numbers well past what this crew currently has — hundreds of millions to billions of ISK per structure, and capital hulls need skill investment on a similar scale to everything else this session combined, per pilot. Worth knowing exists, not worth pricing out yet.
