Arkonor, Bistot, Crokite and Mercoxit don't spawn in highsec at all. That's the whole argument for tagging along on filament runs — not "null pays more for the same rocks," but access to ore types that simply aren't available anywhere the group currently operates.

Riding along on a filament run

The explorers scan and hack; Cody works the belts in the same system. No coordination needed beyond being in the same place, and nobody's waiting on anybody.

The safety edge over wormhole gas: nullsec has local chat. A Venture sitting in a belt sees a neutral enter local at the same instant everyone else does — that's free warning the wormhole version never gives you. Combined with the Venture's built-in warp core strength bonus, getting out is genuinely achievable rather than hopeful.

The catch: a filament drops you somewhere random in the low-activity pool. Whether that system's belts are actually worth mining is luck, not planning. Some trips he'll have nothing to do — same as the explorers landing in a picked-clean system.

The Venture already has a +2 warp core strength role bonus built in, so it beats a single point without spending a low slot on a stabiliser — and it has a dedicated 5,000 m³ ore hold separate from cargo. Lose it and you're out pocket change, which is exactly the right posture for null.

Procurer — the tanky barge

~30–40M all-in · when the system's worth staying in
  • HModulated Strip Miner II — Mining Barge I already trained
  • MLarge Shield Extender I
  • MMultispectrum Shield Hardener I
  • MSurvey Scanner I
  • LDamage Control II

Of the three barges, the Procurer has the strongest tank by a wide margin — the Retriever and Covetor trade that away for yield and ore hold. In null, tank is the one that keeps the ship. Slower to fill than a Covetor, but it survives a bad moment instead of instantly dying to it.

Which to bring: Venture on any filament run where the plan is "look, mine what's there, leave." Procurer only if the group's committed to sitting in one system for a while and the belts justify it — a barge is a much bigger loss and much slower to align out.

Gas huffing — no training needed

The lowest-effort earner on this page, and he can do it today. Gas Cloud Harvesting III is already trained, so a Venture with gas scoops works in lowsec and wormhole gas sites right now — no new skills, no new hull.

Why it suits him: same risk posture as tagging along on a filament run, but it doesn't depend on the group going anywhere. Lowsec gas sites are scannable solo, and the Venture's built-in warp core strength bonus plus a small signature make it genuinely survivable if someone shows up. Lose it and it's pocket change.

The catch: gas sites need scanning down, and his Astrometrics is only III with Rangefinding and Pinpointing untrained — he'll find sites, just slower than a dedicated explorer would. Worth a few days of scanning skills if this becomes a regular thing.

Prospect

the one that travels with you

The covert ops cloak is the whole argument — it moves through hostile space on the same terms as the Asteros and Cheetahs. On a filament run where the group relocates, that means Cody isn't the one ship that can't hide. It also keeps the gas bonus, so it does double duty for huffing.

A full 15,000 m³ compresses to roughly 227 m³ on the Endurance — the Prospect has no compression, so its smaller hold fills and needs hauling out more often.

Endurance

the better solo lowsec earner

No cloaked warping, but its role bonus removes the usual velocity penalty while cloaked and cuts reactivation to 5 seconds. The practical pattern players describe is cloak → align → decloak → warp — "usually only one click away from safety."

One miner running it in low and null reported mining a considerable amount of ice in dangerous systems without fleet support, needing only to stay alert. Bigger hold plus compression means far fewer dangerous return trips, which is the real safety gain.

Honest split: Prospect if he's mostly coming along on group trips. Endurance if he's mostly going out alone. If it's genuinely both, Prospect first — mobility with the group is harder to substitute than hold size.

Deep Core Mining I is the standout — roughly 1,250 SP, effectively free, and it's the difference between being able to touch the single most valuable ore in nullsec or not. Everything else on this list is incremental; that one is a hard gate.

Mining Frigate V is the big one. It's the single gate on both Expedition Frigates — train it and he can choose Prospect or Endurance depending on how the group is actually playing at the time, rather than committing now.
