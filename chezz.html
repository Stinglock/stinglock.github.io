What's actually confirmed

All 8 Gallente ship skills trained, Battleship at V — that's the ceiling on the racial ship tree fully maxed. Zero Minmatar ship skills, which means none of Stinglock's own hulls (Hurricane, Loki, Maelstrom, etc.) are usable by Chezz — no swapping into a spare ship from that hangar.

Full drone tree at V/IV across the board is a serious investment — but Heavy Drone Operation is only I and Sentry Drone Interfacing is untrained. That caps him at medium drones (Hammerhead II) as the practical ceiling right now, despite the rest of the drone tree being nearly complete. Gunnery sits at a thin 12/65 and Armor at 8/14 — guns and armor tanking are both underdeveloped next to the drone investment.

What that skill shape points at

This section is extrapolated from trained skills, not a confirmed fit or hull list — there's no asset data on file for Chezz at all, so treat every hull name below as "would be usable," not "is owned."

Dominix — the obvious fit

Gallente Battleship V, drone-bonused hull

The Dominix is the textbook drone battleship and Battleship V means it's flown at full bonus. The catch is the same one showing up everywhere on this page: the Dominix wants sentry drones for its best PvE ratting fits, and Sentry Drone Interfacing is untrained — so it'd be running on Hammerhead IIs instead of Bouncers or Curators. Still very usable, just leaving damage on the table against its ideal setup.

Myrmidon / Ishtar — medium alternative

matches current drone ceiling exactly

Both hulls are built around medium drones, which is exactly where the trained skills currently sit — no wasted potential the way the Dominix has. The Ishtar specifically is a strong solo/small-gang drone cruiser if PvP ever comes up; the Myrmidon covers similar ground a size class up with more tank.

With Gunnery at only 12/65, any of these fits should be leaning on drones for damage and using guns as a light backup at best — that ratio isn't close to flipping any time soon.

Where he fits with the rest of the group

Chezz and Miko's SP sit within a few million of each other, but in completely different racial lines with essentially no overlap into Minmatar — neither can hop into a spare Rifter or Rupture from Stinglock's hangar. That makes Chezz's Gallente drone boats a genuine third damage type and playstyle in the group rather than a duplicate of what's already flying, which is worth more than it might look like on paper.
