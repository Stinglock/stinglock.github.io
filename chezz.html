Corrections to the previous version of this page

The old page reported 15.71M SP — the live export shows 20.71M, making Chezz the highest-SP pilot in the group rather than roughly level with Miko. It also said Heavy Drone Operation was I and Sentry Drone Interfacing untrained; both are now III. Most importantly, it claimed "guns and armor tanking are both underdeveloped" off a 12/65 Gunnery category count. That was misleading — category coverage counts every laser and projectile skill he'll never train. In reality he has every hybrid turret size at V plus Weapon Upgrades V and Advanced Weapon Upgrades V. His gunnery isn't thin, it's specialised and maxed.

What he's actually good at

Gunnery — maxed, not thin

all three hybrid sizes at V

Gunnery V. Small, Medium and Large Hybrid Turret all V. Weapon Upgrades V and Advanced Weapon Upgrades V — roughly 2M SP of pure fitting headroom, so he can fit loadouts most pilots can't squeeze onto a hull. Surgical Strike, Rapid Firing, Sharpshooter, Motion Prediction and Trajectory Analysis all at IV.

Drones — deepest on the roster

two soft spots only

Drones V, Light and Medium Drone Operation V, Drone Avionics V, Drone Navigation V, Gallente Drone Specialization IV (T2 Gallente drones), plus Drone Interfacing, Advanced Drone Avionics, Drone Durability and Drone Sharpshooting all at IV. Heavy Drone Operation III and Sentry Drone Interfacing III are the only weak links.

He and Cody overlap properly

This is the only genuine role redundancy on the roster. Chezz has better Astrogeology (V vs III) and a far better barge (Mining Barge IV vs I); Cody has better Reprocessing (V vs IV) and dedicated gas harvesting. Two miners who can cover for each other rather than one irreplaceable specialist. Ship and site options for both on Mining.

Where he fits with the rest of the group

Zero Minmatar ship skills — none of Stinglock's hulls are usable by Chezz, and that runs both ways. What he brings instead is a genuinely different damage type and playstyle: Gallente drone and hybrid-turret boats, at a higher SP level than anyone else on the roster.

The roster problem he highlights: nobody except Stinglock can hack. Chezz has Archaeology and Hacking at zero, Miko is training Hacking V, Cody has neither. On a filament or wormhole run Chezz currently has no exploration role — he'd come along as escort or as a second miner, not as another pair of hands on the cans.
