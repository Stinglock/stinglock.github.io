Playbook Variations

The base playbook is the safe mission-running default. These are the swaps if Stinglock wants more damage or EWar leaning toward PvP later, and a full-drone rework for Chezz.

Stinglock — Stabber variants

All still 4 high / 4 mid / 6 low, 3 rigs — module swaps against the base fit on the playbook, not new hulls. The Stabber has no EWar bonus, so the EWar option below is a compromise, not a real EWar boat — see the dedicated hulls further down for that.

Variant 1

Damage-max

Best for L1/L2, nothing shoots back hard
  • Low3–4× Gyrostabilizer II — up from 2
  • Mid10MN MWD — swap from AB, range control on gank runs
  • DropTracking Computer + Tracking Enhancer

Loses tank and cap stability, gains flat DPS. Bad idea once tanking L3+ solo.

Variant 2

EWar / support

Compromise fit — useful in a duo, not solo
  • HiDrop 1–2 turrets for utility
  • MidTarget Painter — helps fleetmate's tracking
  • MidStasis Webifier — or this, for tackle

The real answer to EWar is the Rapier — force recon, webs + points, EWar bonuses baked in. Locked behind Minmatar Cruiser V (~19d), same as the Loki.

Variant 3

PvP-lean, now

Don't touch the Stabber — use a frigate instead

Use the Slasher or Condor instead — both already flyable, both proper tackle frigates, and losing one doesn't hurt like losing the Stabber does.

  • MidWarp Disruptor
  • MidStasis Webifier
  • LowCheap plate or nothing — align time over tank

Basically Path B from the fleet page pulled forward to today instead of waiting on Assault Frigates.

Jaguar/Rapier/Hound/Loki are the real dedicated PvP hulls but sit behind level-V-adjacent walls weeks out. Nothing new needs buying — it's all sitting in the hangar or the queue already.

Myrmidon — full drone

Drop the turrets, go all-in on the drone bonus
  • Hi2× Salvager I
  • Hi4× empty or Drone Link Augmentor I — extends control range for kiting
  • Mid10MN Afterburner I
  • Mid2× Cap Recharger II
  • Mid2× Omnidirectional Tracking Link I — drone tracking/optimal, stacks
  • LowMedium Armor Repairer II
  • LowDamage Control II
  • Low3× Drone Damage Amplifier II — up from 2
  • Rig2× Medium Auxiliary Nano Pump I, 1× Drone Control Range Augmentor I — unchanged

Trade: no gun damage at all if drones get neuted or killed, but with 200m³ bay and 3 DDAs it out-damages the mixed fit against anything that isn't actively popping drones.
