Reading the "insure?" column

  • yesInsurance payout covers roughly half or more of the hull's replacement cost. Cheap premium, real offset — insure it.
  • marginalPayout covers under a third of the hull. Still cheap to buy, but don't count on it to soften a loss.
  • skipCCP's insurance formula treats T3 and faction hulls very differently from T1/T2 — the Loki's payout is ~10% of its hull value, the Astero's is under 1%. Not worth the trip to a station to buy it.
