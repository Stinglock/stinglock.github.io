The asymmetry

Four pilots, four completely different shapes

Coreun is the highest-SP pilot at 20.7M — a maxed Gallente drone/gunnery battleship pilot with a serious mining side, but zero T2 hulls and no exploration skills at all. Stinglock at 18.0M is the only one with Minmatar hulls and the only one flying wormhole content, and funds the operation. Miko at 14.7M is a Caldari missile/shield pilot whose Raven could plausibly solo C3s. Cody at 2.3M is tiny by comparison but maxed exactly where it counts for mining.

The practical problem: almost no hull overlap. Nobody can hop into anyone else's spare ship. Coreun and Cody both mine, which is the only genuine redundancy on the roster. Nobody except Stinglock can hack a can.
