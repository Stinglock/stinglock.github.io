Future path options — if the plan branches

The rebuilt plan already leans mission-runner-first (§10 gameplay door-openers cover trade, industry, exploration and combat support broadly), but three later blocks are really separate specialisations bolted onto the same base. Matches the fleet paths on the fleet page.

Stay the course — mission runner

Everything through block 11 (turret support to IV) is mission-runner infrastructure. Stop there and you're fully kitted for L1–L4s without touching the level-V gate at all.

Branch into PvP

Block 13's Assault Frigates 1 and Recon Ships 1 unlock the Jaguar and Rapier respectively — both already owned. Electronic Warfare and Propulsion Jamming (block 10) are already queued and double as tackle/support skills.

Branch into exploration

Astrometrics, Hacking, Archaeology and Survey (block 10) are already in the plan — they're what make the five owned exploration frigates actually productive, not just flyable.

Verify before acting. A skill absent from the queue is either already trained or untrained — the queue alone can't tell you which, only live skill data can. This caused three wrong findings in the source session (see the corrections log in the original handoff).
