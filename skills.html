Gaps found, checked against a real Hurricane pilot's skill benchmark

Cross-checked the current trained levels against Captain Benzi's "what I'd recommend before undocking a Hurricane" breakdown (see the full table below) and against the original plan. Four skills come up short of what a Hurricane actually wants:

Surgical Strike (rank 4, flat turret damage %) and Trajectory Analysis (rank 5, turret optimal range) are both sitting at rank 1 trained and aren't in the live queue at all — already flagged last pass, and the benchmark confirms both are worth training to at least IV. Minmatar Battlecruiser is at III; the benchmark's own line is "I wouldn't even bother undocking this really before getting that at least to level four," and it's not in the live queue either. Medium Projectile Turret is at III; the benchmark runs IV today and is training toward V + specializations for T2 ammo access — also absent from the queue. Recommended insertion point for all four: after Weapon Upgrades IV, before Mechanics V, ahead of the level-V gate and the entire Loki/Jaguar/Rapier/HAC tail behind it, since none of that back half does anything for a Hurricane.
