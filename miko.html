Weapons & support

This is a real missile pilot, not a generalist who happens to own missile boats. Missile Launcher Operation V, Cruise Missiles IV, Heavy Missiles IV, Heavy Assault Missiles IV, Light Missiles IV, Rapid Launch IV — every missile size is trained deep, which is exactly what the Raven/Caracal/Drake trio needs.

Gunnery V and Drones V are both maxed as support skills — but that's support only. Small Hybrid Turret is just III, so there's no real turret specialization to lean on if a fit calls for guns instead of launchers. Shield Management V and Shield Operation V confirm the shield-tank lean, which lines up with the rest of the group's Minmatar shield doctrine even though the hulls don't overlap.

What he can actually fly and fight in

Raven — L4 cruise missile

A solid mid-tier L4 fit — four BCS is a heavy damage stack, and the XL shield booster plus MJD gives a genuine escape option Sleeper or Angel content both respect. The gap: no Cruise Missile Specialization yet, so damage application is a step below what the skills otherwise support.

Drake / Caracal — HAM secondary

flyable, untested fit

Heavy Assault Missiles at IV means a Drake or Caracal HAM fit is genuinely competitive right now, not just theoretically flyable. The Drake in particular is a cheap, tanky battlecruiser that could absorb a lot of the same L3/L4 workload as the Raven at a fraction of the hull cost — worth having as the "don't risk the Raven" option.

The Worm is the interesting outlier: a faction cruiser with a drone bonus, and Drones V means it'd actually use that bonus properly. Could double as a light solo PvP hull if that direction ever comes up.

Queue signal

Training toward Minmatar Frigate V, then Hacking V, Cruise Missiles V, Advanced Weapon Upgrades IV, Target Painting III, Light Drone Operation V.

The Minmatar Frigate push is odd. Zero Minmatar hulls currently owned, against a Caldari-heavy hangar with real missile investment behind it — worth asking him directly what that's queued for before assuming it's a mistake. Could be prep for a shared Rifter/Rupture with the rest of the group, could be a Recon/exploration frigate plan, or could just be a leftover from an old queue.

Everything after that lines up cleanly with the existing build: Cruise Missiles V closes the specialization gap above, Hacking V and Light Drone Operation V both point at relic/data or exploration content, which the Astero would be perfect for once Gallente Frigate finally gets trained.
