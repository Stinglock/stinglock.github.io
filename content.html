Already queued · block 10

Planetary Interaction

Interplanetary Consolidation 2, Command Center Upgrades 2

Set up 2–3 planets, extract raw materials, run the processing chain, sell or feed into manufacturing. Genuinely passive once set up — refresh extractors every day or two. Low-to-mid ISK/hr but scales with more planets and command centers.

low effort, low risk
Already queued · block 7

Market trading

Trade 4, Daytrading, Marketing, Broker Relations, Accounting, Contracting

Place remote sell/buy orders from Rens without traveling, undercut margins, let orders fill while offline. Not passive in the AFK sense, but no undocking or combat risk. With 1.81B sitting idle in the wallet, this is the best use of that capital — it earns nothing parked.

best use of idle capital

Reprocessing + selling minerals

Reprocessing 2 already queued (block 6). Salvage/loot from the L1/L2 grind is currently just vendored (see the sell-list action item on the playbook). Reprocess what's reprocessable instead, sell the minerals — marginal but free.

Research agents / science

Laboratory Operation 2 and Research 2 are queued (block 6) but no other science skills exist yet. Unlocks datacore farming, feeds invention/T2 production later. Bigger investment — only worth it if manufacturing is a real goal.

Skip: moon mining / structure rental

Needs null/lowsec exposure and an anchored structure. Doesn't fit a highsec two-man op.

Realistic ranking

PI first — skills already queued, genuinely passive, no risk. Market trading second — puts the 1.81B to work instead of sitting idle. Reprocessing is free money on the side. Research agents only if T2 manufacturing is a real goal down the line.
